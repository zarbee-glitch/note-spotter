Guitar music is written an octave above how it sounds, so the app listens for the sounding pitch: the E in the top space is the open 1st string. Pluck one string at a time and let it ring. If you play a different note, a grey note shows on the stave where your note is written. Tune your guitar first, and play somewhere fairly quiet.
